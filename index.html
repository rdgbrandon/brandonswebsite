01 / ABOUT

Intelligence is more useful
when we can understand it.

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning and interpretability. I work with Dr. Sumit Jha at the University of Florida, studying neuro-symbolic methods and developing AI and interpretability prototypes for DARPA-sponsored research.

Before UF, I completed my M.S. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence at Florida International University through its accelerated pathway. My work spans financial forecasting, conditional video understanding, and explaining what vision-language models attend to.

A common thread: making a model’s reasoning easier to inspect, question, and improve.

01 / ABOUT

About me.

I’m a graduate research assistant in Dr. Sumit Jha’s lab at UF and an AI engineer on DARPA-sponsored research. I develop interpretability methods, run experiments, and build prototypes to share our results.

I earned my B.S. in Computer Science and a minor in Mathematics at Florida International University, followed by an M.S. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence through FIU’s accelerated pathway. My master’s thesis, advised by Dr. Yanzhao Wu, focused on conditional video question answering.

Online-Adaptive Fuzzy Cognitive Maps for Neuro-symbolic Financial Forecasting

I combine fuzzy cognitive maps with an LSTM and news sentiment for financial forecasting. The maps update as new data arrives, so we can examine how the relationships between variables change over time.

CondVQA: A Benchmark for Conditional Visual Question Answering in Video Understanding

My master’s thesis examines questions about specific moments in a video. The pipeline separates the condition from the question and uses it to find relevant frames before answering. Advised by Dr. Yanzhao Wu.
